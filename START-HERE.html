One full upgrade. Small updates later.

Upload this edition's files together once, including content.js, motion.js and editor-content.js. Later, upload exported data.js and any new images.

04 / KEEP THE PICTURES

Assets travel with the site.

Keep assets/ beside index.html. Image paths are case-sensitive. A data export contains paths, not image files.
